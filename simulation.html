Temperature study explorer

Reactor outlet temperature versus plant duties and flows, from my Aspen Plus V11 model of a simplified ethylene–isobutane to n-octane process.

Read this before the charts

The reactor uses a specified 80% ethylene conversion, not temperature-dependent kinetics. That is why octane output does not move. This explorer shows how the simulated plant responds; it does not show how temperature affects the real reaction.
